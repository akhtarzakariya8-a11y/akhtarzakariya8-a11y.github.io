Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who this covers

Wazir is a private assistant used only by its owner. It has no other users. This policy covers the Google account data Wazir can reach when its owner signs in with Google.

What Wazir accesses

With the owner's permission, Wazir can read and organise the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets, and save email drafts. It does not send email, post, delete or change account settings unless the owner asks it to.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner with their own tasks, such as summarising their inbox, planning their day or finding a document. It is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models by Wazir.

Where it is kept

Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's private server. Parts of the owner's data may be sent to the AI services the owner uses (such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and xAI) to answer the owner's requests, under those services' own terms.

Removing access

The owner can remove Wazir's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Wazir's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
